# MarketThread

> AI-powered global market intelligence.

MarketThread is a financial intelligence platform designed to connect global events, financial news, market data, company fundamentals, market behavior, and machine-learning signals into explainable market insights.

## Core Concept

```text
World Events
     ↓
News & Information
     ↓
Event Detection
     ↓
Market Impact
     ↓
Company Impact
     ↓
Signals
     ↓
Risk + Confidence
     ↓
Investment Insight
