# MarketThread Product Specification

MarketThread is an AI-powered financial intelligence platform that analyzes global information and market data to help users understand potential market implications.

The product connects events occurring in the world with their potential effects on financial markets, industries, companies, and portfolios.

The central product flow is:

```text
World Event
     ↓
Information
     ↓
Event
     ↓
Market Impact
     ↓
Company Impact
     ↓
Evidence
     ↓
Signal
     ↓
Risk + Confidence
     ↓
Investment Insight
